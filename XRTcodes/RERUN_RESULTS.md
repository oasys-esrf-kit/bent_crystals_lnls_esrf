# Rerun after the geometry and ML fixes (2026-10-28)

All five scripts were rerun over their full energy scans with the geometry fix
(`BUG_GEOMETRY.md`) and the three ML fixes (`BUG_ML.md`). The two Bragg+Laue beamlines
were also run in a test configuration that is **not** in the scripts (see "Test runs").

`RUN_XRT.md` has been updated with these results and keeps the old ones in its section
"Results before the fixes". Its TODO list repeats the open decisions below.

## Summary

- **Double-Laue beamlines (SSU_SBDL, BNL_SBDL): the results are usable.**
  - SSU_SBDL (PP): flux ×1.16–1.52 the old value, Fcurv 0.94–0.99 (was 1.05–1.11),
    horizontal focus at 55.6–56.3 m for a 56 m screen. Horizontal FWHM is similar
    (34–84 µm); the energy bandwidth is 15–36% smaller above 50 keV.
  - BNL_SBDL (ML): flux ×0.25 at 30–40 keV and ×0.94–1.07 at 50–70 keV. Fcurv 1.005–1.03
    (was 1.18–1.20). Horizontal FWHM 0.40–0.46 mm (was 0.24–0.30 mm). The large drop at
    30–40 keV was not investigated: the geometry fix alone gave ×2 at 40 keV
    (`oasys_BNL_SBDL/INFO.md`), so the ML fix accounts for about ×0.12 there.
- **Bragg+Laue beamlines (SSU_BLM, SSU_BLM_PP, APS_BLM): the scripts' results are not
  usable.**
  - Flux drops to 10⁻⁴–10⁻³ of the old values. Sizes and bandwidths are meaningless
    (horizontal FWHM up to 0.4 mm, Fcurv stuck at the edge of the search range at low E for
    SSU_BLM).
  - **Cause:** with absorption now active in the Bragg ML reflectivity, the Bragg and Laue
    reflection windows no longer overlap. The internal model centres the crystal depth on
    the mid-plane (`PP_center_fraction` is ignored), which shifts the reflection profiles
    by about 160 µrad in the thick Bragg crystal. Before the ML fix the Bragg crystal
    reflected R ≈ 1 over 330 µrad, which hid the shift. The scripts align the Laue crystal
    at the nominal angle and never rock it.
- **Test runs (entrance-surface reference + Laue rocking): flux 0.06–0.21× old (SSU_BLM)
  and 0.16–0.36× old (APS_BLM), with a well-behaved focus.** See below.

## Test runs (not in the scripts)

The wrapper `test_fc0_rock/run_fc0_rock.py` runs the unmodified script with two changes.
Run it from the output folder: `python ../test_fc0_rock/run_fc0_rock.py XRT_SSU_BLM`.

1. **Entrance-surface reference:** `test_fc0_rock/BentCrys2D.py`, a copy of `BentCrys2D.py`
   that honours `PP_center_fraction` (the change is in `test_fc0_rock/PP_center_fraction.diff`),
   with the parameter forced to 0 for every crystal. The PP and ML profiles then start at the entrance
   surface, as in XOPPY. Against XOPPY at 80 keV the profile positions agree within 0.1
   µrad (Laue ML [−0.6, 43.4] vs [−0.5, 43.3] µrad; PP [0.2, 43.5] vs [0.3, 43.5]).
2. **Rocking the Laue crystal:** before each energy, the Laue pitch is scanned from −60 to
   +160 µrad in 5 µrad steps (100k rays, Fcurv = 1) and set to the offset of maximum
   intensity. Then the script's own Fcurv optimization and full run follow.

Results:

- **SSU_BLM:** the best offset grows with energy, from 0 at 35 keV to +120 µrad at
  200 keV. Rocking increases the intensity by ×1–4 below 60 keV, ×8–14 at 60–85 keV and
  ×20–30 from 90 keV. Fcurv is 0.999–1.002 at all energies and the horizontal focus is
  at 55.9–56.7 m. Horizontal FWHM 26–60 µm (old 26–64 µm), vertical 0.5–1.0 mm (old
  2.3–8.2 mm).
- **APS_BLM:** best offset +5 to +30 µrad, gain ×1–4. Fcurv 0.996–1.003, horizontal focus
  52.6–53.0 m for a 52 m screen.
- **The test flux is still well below the old values.** The remaining factor mainly
  comes from the Bragg crystal's ML reflectivity with xrt's absorption coefficient: 17
  µrad integrated at 80 keV against 67 µrad in XOPPY (photoelectric μ). With XOPPY's μ the
  internal code gives 71 µrad (`BUG_ML.md`).
- **The double-Laue beamlines depend only weakly on the depth reference:** at one energy
  each, fc = 0 changed the intensity by −20% (SSU_SBDL) and +4% (BNL_SBDL).

## Open decisions

1. **Depth reference:** implement `PP_center_fraction` in `BentCrys2D.py` and
   `bent_crys_2d.py` and use 0 (entrance surface, as XOPPY) in the scripts, the widget
   default and the `.ows` files.
2. **Second-crystal alignment:** add a per-energy rocking scan of the Laue crystal to
   `XRT_SSU_BLM.py`, `XRT_SSU_BLM_PP.py` and `XRT_APS_BLM.py`, as in the test wrapper.
3. **Absorption coefficient:** keep xrt's total μ (current) or use the photoelectric μ as
   XOPPY. This changes the Bragg integrated reflectivity by about ×4
   (17 vs 71 µrad at 80 keV); the effect on the flux was not traced.
